Prepared for The Kikar Lodge  |  Date: 22 May 2026

1Where We Are & Why Phase 2

In Phase 1 we centralised all your WhatsApp and call leads into Zoho CRM so nothing is lost and data keeps growing. It works well for leads — but reservations still live in Excel on the side, and the Professional plan does not allow the customisations needed to fix that. Phase 2 closes that gap and adds the intelligence layer on top.

2What We Will Build

Four focused deliverables — each tied directly to a problem you face today.

Enterprise Plan Upgrade

Move your account from Professional to Enterprise to unlock the customisation engine (custom modules, functions, validations, custom views).

Solves: The plan ceiling that currently blocks any reservation build.

Customised Reservation Module

A dedicated reservations module with a calendar-style availability view. If a room is booked for a date range, the system blocks it from being assigned to anyone else for the same dates.

Solves: Removes Excel — bookings and double-booking checks live inside CRM.

Custom Dashboards & Reports

Decision-ready dashboards — leads, conversions, occupancy and reservation trends — in one live view for your team and management.

Solves: Faster, clearer decisions without manual report-pulling.

AI WhatsApp Bot Training

Your connected WhatsApp AI bot is trained to respond conversationally to guest enquiries and feed qualified leads straight into the CRM.

Solves: Responsive 24/7 enquiry handling that grows your lead pipeline.

3Implementation Includes

  • Customised Reservation module with availability calendar & double-booking prevention
  • Custom dashboards & reports configured to your KPIs
  • AI WhatsApp bot training & CRM lead routing
  • On-site training for your team
