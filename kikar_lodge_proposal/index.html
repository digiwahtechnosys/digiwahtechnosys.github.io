Zoho MCP — your property, through Claude

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is the standard that lets an AI assistant connect directly to live business software. We configure Claude against your existing Zoho subscriptions, so you can simply ask — from your desk, or from your phone at the other end of the country.

Reads live data straight from CRM, Books, Inventory and POS — never a stale export
Runs inside your existing Zoho permissions, so it sees only what that user is allowed to see
Answers in plain language, across systems, in one question
Works from your phone — you stay in charge of the property while you are away from it
