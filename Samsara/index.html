My Operator IVR — Connected to Zoho CRM

Every call that comes in on your IVR today disappears after it rings. With Zoho CRM integration, every call is captured, logged, and acted on — automatically. Missed a call from a hot lead? CRM creates a callback task and notifies your team. Picked up a call? The guest's profile pops up on screen so your team knows exactly who's calling and what their last interaction was.
