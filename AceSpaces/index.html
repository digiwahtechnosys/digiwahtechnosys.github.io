01 — The platform

Three layers, one connected system

Each layer owns its own job. Nothing is entered twice.

CRM

Zoho CRM

Sales & client operations

Every lead, deal, client, membership, cabin, pass and visitor. The admin team's home screen — and the master record for who is in which space.

BKS

Zoho Books

Billing & accounting

GST invoicing, recurring monthly billing, payment reminders, vendor management and purchase orders. Fed automatically from CRM — no re-keying.

APP

Ace Spaces App

Member self-service

A custom web app where members book meeting and conference rooms, track their credit balance, and raise issues — without calling the front desk.

Why a custom app for booking? Room booking needs instant availability and guaranteed protection against two people taking the same slot — that's a job for a purpose-built application. The admin views, including the floor plan, are built directly into Zoho CRM as custom screens, so the team works in one place.

03 — Zoho CRM

Sales and client operations

Everything about a client, reachable from one screen.

L Lead management

Website forms, Meta lead ads, IVR calls, WhatsApp and walk-ins all capture into one place, tagged by centre and routed to the right person.

P Sales pipelines

Separate pipelines for cabin/office sales, day & week passes and virtual office — each with its own stages. Centre is a filter, so any pipeline can be viewed per location.

A Follow-up automation

Automatic reminders, task creation and WhatsApp/email sequences so no enquiry goes cold because someone forgot.

M Membership management

Every active contract with its seats, term, rate, deposit, lock-in and renewal date — surviving across renewals so history is never lost.

C Cabin & space inventory

Every cabin, desk and room with live status. "What's free on 1st October" becomes a one-click answer instead of a phone call.

D Day & week passes

A fast front-desk console to sell a pass, check someone in, and see today's occupancy against capacity.

V Visitor management

Log visitors with host, purpose and check-in/out. Any walk-in enquiry converts to a lead in one click.

S Documents & agreements

Agreements signed digitally through Zoho Sign, with KYC and GST documents stored against the client and expiry reminders built in.

F Feedback automation

Automatic satisfaction surveys after onboarding, on a regular cycle, and when a support issue closes.

04 — Zoho Books

Billing, GST and accounts

Connected to CRM, so client data is entered once.

₹ GST invoicing

Fully GST-compliant invoices with e-invoicing filed directly to the government portal. Bank details printed on every invoice.

↻ Recurring invoices

Monthly membership billing generates and sends itself. Set once when the client joins, then it runs.

→ One-click invoicing

A Create Invoice button inside CRM builds the invoice in Books with all client and line-item detail already filled in.

! Payment reminders

An automatic reminder ladder before due date and after — escalating to the account owner and then the centre manager.

% TDS tracking

Corporate clients deducting TDS on rent are handled properly, so receivables reconcile instead of sitting part-paid forever.

V Vendor portal & POs

Vendors submit invoices and track payment status themselves. Purchase orders and consumables stock managed in one place.

Per-centre reporting. All three centres run under a single GSTIN, so revenue and costs are tagged by centre inside one Books account — giving per-centre P&L without duplicate invoice numbering or extra subscriptions.
05 — The member app

Members book their own rooms

Custom-built. Branded to Ace Spaces. Works on phone and desktop.

📅 Live booking calendar

Members see real availability and book a slot in seconds. Booked slots disappear instantly for everyone else — no double bookings, ever.

⏱ Per-seat credits

Each seat earns 5 hours meeting room and 3 hours conference room per month, pooled across the client's team. Credits deduct automatically on booking and reset monthly.

🔧 Issues tab

Members raise an issue with a photo and priority; it lands with the admin team as a tracked ticket, and the member can follow its status.

👥 Team management

Each client company gets an admin who can see team usage, invite colleagues and manage their own users.

A live map, not a static drawing

Every cabin, desk and room on the plan is connected to its record. Colours update themselves as clients join, give notice or move out — so the floor plan is always current without anyone maintaining it.

Click any space to see who's in it, what they pay and when their term ends. Pick a future date and the plan shows what the floor will look like then — which is what turns "do we have room for a 10-seater in October?" into a two-second answer.

Editable by your team

Layouts change. Cabins get split, merged, repurposed. The plan includes a simple drag-and-drop editor so the admin team can redraw the floor themselves — add a cabin, resize it, move a wall, relabel a room — without coming back to us.

Shapes snap to a grid, each one links to its cabin record, and changes save instantly. One plan per floor, per centre.

360 Client 360 panel

Open any client and see membership, cabins, credit balance, invoices, open issues and signed documents in a single panel — without clicking into five different places.

✎ Layout editor

Draw and edit the floor plan for each centre — add cabins, resize, relabel and link each space to its record. No technical help needed.

🎫 Pass console

Sell a day or week pass in seconds, check members in, and see today's footfall against capacity per centre.

📋 Booking overview

Every room booking across all centres for the day, with the ability to block a room or grant extra credits.

07 — Business intelligence

Numbers that drive decisions

Dashboards combining CRM, Books and booking data.

Occupancy

Occupancy percentage per centre, vacant desks, average days a cabin sits empty, and the trend over time.

Revenue

Monthly recurring revenue, average revenue per client, revenue per square foot, and billed vs collected.

Sales

Conversion at each pipeline stage, which lead sources actually close, and how long the sales cycle really takes.

Retention

Churn rate, renewals due, satisfaction trend, and an at-risk list combining low usage, open issues and late payment.

Room utilisation

Peak booking hours, which rooms are over- or under-used, and how much of the credit allowance clients actually consume.

Operations

Issue volume and resolution time per centre, plus visitor footfall.

10 — Delivery

Four to five weeks to live

Sales goes live in week two, so the team gets value before the full build finishes.

1

Foundations · Week 1

Zoho One setup, users and roles, three centres configured, GST and item catalogue, WhatsApp templates prepared.

2

Sales live · Week 1–2

Leads, pipelines, follow-up automation, and capture from website, Meta, IVR and WhatsApp. Sales team starts using the system here.

3

Client operations · Week 2–3

Memberships, cabins, passes, visitors, documents and agreements. Admin team moves off spreadsheets.

4

Finance live · Week 3–4

Books configured, CRM integration, recurring billing, e-invoicing, reminders and vendor portal.

5

Member app · Week 2–4 — parallel track

Booking engine, credit system, issues, team management, and the admin consoles inside CRM. Runs alongside the Zoho build rather than after it.

6

Dashboards & handover · Week 4–5

Analytics dashboards, feedback automation, team training and documentation.

How this timeline works. The member app is built as a parallel track from week two, once the membership structure is locked — so the Zoho configuration and the application progress at the same time instead of one waiting on the other. Each phase ends with a working demo. Timelines assume centre details, GST information and the plan catalogue are provided in week one.
