2

Team Confirms in the Order Panel — A UI They Already Know

We rebuild your existing Order Panel as a custom screen inside Zoho CRM. Same columns — Order Date, Client Name, Mobile, Lead Source, Shipping details, Status. Same filters. Same Excel/CSV/Print exports. Training cost: near zero.

3

Confirmed Order Ships Itself

On confirmation, CRM checks pincode serviceability, pushes the order to the right courier, receives the tracking number (AWB), and attaches a ready-to-print shipping label — all in seconds.

No more double-entering orders into the Delhivery app
Undeliverable pincodes are caught before packing effort is spent
If a push ever fails, it is flagged in the panel for one-click retry — never silent
⚡ AWB + label generated automatically
4

Delivery Status Updates Itself — The End of Courier-Website Checking

Every scan the courier records — picked up, in transit, out for delivery, delivered — flows straight into CRM and updates the Delivery Status column live. An hourly safety sweep double-checks every open shipment so nothing is ever missed.

Full scan history stored on every order for audit and disputes
One consistent status language across both couriers
💬 WhatsApp → Customer at each delivery milestone (see Capability 3)
5

Delivered — And Reported
