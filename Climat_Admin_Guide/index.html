1 Overview

3 The daily flow

The four things that happen every day, in order.

A · How jobs arrive

When someone in Unleashed sets a sales order's Custom Order Status to DELIVERY, the system automatically creates a matching Delivery Job in the app (status Unassigned), pulling in the customer, address and line items.

  • Automatic: the app checks Unleashed for new delivery orders every 10 minutes — no action needed.
  • On demand: if you want a job to appear immediately, open the Dispatch Board and click ⟳ Sync from Unleashed.
💡
A job only auto-creates once per sales order (no duplicates). Editing the order in Unleashed after the job exists won't change the existing job.

B · Dispatching on the board

Open the Dispatch Board (a calendar of drivers × time slots, with an Unassigned rail on the left).

  1. Drag a job from the Unassigned rail onto a driver column and the time slot you want it delivered.
  2. The job's status becomes Assigned and the assigned driver is instantly notified on their phone (see §3C).
  3. To re-assign, just drag the card to a different driver/time. To refresh the board, use Refresh or Sync.
ℹ️
Assign jobs on the Dispatch Board wherever possible — it's built for it and keeps everyone's view consistent.

C · The driver's job

When a driver is assigned a job, they get an in-app notification ("New Delivery Assigned — Job SO-… for [Customer]") in the Notifications tab of their Portal app. Tapping it opens the job.

  1. Driver opens My Deliveries — their run sheet, sorted by time. They only see jobs assigned to them.
  2. They tap the address to navigate (opens Google Maps).
  3. On arrival they open Job Completion: take a POD photo (camera), capture the customer's signature, and confirm quantities delivered per line.
  4. They submit — done.

D · Completion & write-back

On submit, the system updates the job status and pushes the result back to Unleashed automatically:

  • If everything was delivered → Delivered.
  • If only part was delivered → Part Delivered (stays active for the remainder).
  • Unleashed's sales order is marked Shipped and a dated Comments note records the driver, items and quantities delivered.
💡
This is the payoff: proof of delivery reaches Unleashed the moment the driver submits — no paperwork, no chasing, faster invoicing.

4 Managing drivers

A driver needs two things: a Driver record (so they appear on the board) and a portal login (so they can use the app).

Add a driver record

  1. Go to Drivers → All Drivers → + Add.
  2. Enter Name, Phone (Australian, +61), Email, Vehicle Rego & Type, and tick Active.
⚠️
The driver's email must exactly match the email they log into the app with. This is how the system shows each driver only their own jobs. A mismatch = the driver sees nothing.

Give the driver app access

  1. Invite them as a Portal user with the Driver permission (same email as their Driver record).
  2. They accept the invite, install the Zoho Creator Portal app, and log in.
  3. They'll land on My Deliveries.

5 Managing office & dispatch users

Office and dispatch staff are added as app users (not portal users).

  1. From the app, open Users and add the person by email with the appropriate role.
  2. App users can see all jobs, dispatch on the board, and view reports.
  3. Each app user consumes one Zoho Creator user licence; the same person can use all your apps with one licence.
ℹ️
Need more licences before adding people? Check your Zoho subscription first, or contact Digiwah and we'll advise.

8 Unleashed integration (plain English)

The app talks to Unleashed both ways. You don't need to manage anything technical — just know the two rules.

Inbound (Unleashed → app)

Setting a sales order's Custom Order Status to DELIVERY brings it into the app as a delivery job (automatically every 10 minutes, or instantly via the Sync from Unleashed button).

Outbound (app → Unleashed)

When a driver completes a delivery, the app marks the Unleashed order Shipped and writes a dated proof note in Comments.

⚠️
Two rules to keep it working:
1. The custom status word in Unleashed must be exactly DELIVERY.
2. Do not delete the Climat connection (webhook subscription) in Unleashed during account clean-ups — it's how the two systems stay linked.

9 Troubleshooting & FAQ

Most issues are quick self-fixes. Try these before calling support.

A new order isn't showing up as a job
  • Open the Dispatch Board and click ⟳ Sync from Unleashed.
  • Check the Unleashed order's Custom Order Status is exactly DELIVERY.
  • Remember it's a new order — re-using an old order number won't create a second job.
A driver says they can't see any jobs
  • Check the job is actually Assigned to that driver on the board.
  • Check the Driver record email matches the email they logged in with — this is the usual cause.
A driver can't log into the mobile app
  • They must use the Zoho Creator Portal app, not the standard "Zoho Creator" app.
  • Confirm their portal invite was accepted.
The camera doesn't open for the POD photo
  • POD capture works in the Portal app — tap the POD Photo field and choose Take Photo.
  • Make sure the app has camera permission in the phone's settings.
The dispatch board looks out of date or shows an odd card
  • Click Refresh (or Sync) — the board reconciles with the latest data.
A delivery was only partly fulfilled
  • The driver enters the actual quantities per line on the Job Completion form. The job becomes Part Delivered and stays active for the remainder.
